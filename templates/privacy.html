Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

We do not collect payment information. Spendly is free to use and does not process any financial transactions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the Spendly service.
  • Display your expense history and summaries back to you.
  • Send essential service communications such as password reset emails.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon request, we will remove your data within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We may use trusted service providers (such as hosting infrastructure) who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

Any third-party integrations we add in the future will be disclosed in an updated version of this policy.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies strictly to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request data deletion, please reach out via the contact information provided on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests within 7 business days.
